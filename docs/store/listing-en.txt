A stack of 10,000 ten-thousand-yen bills sits in front of you.
Flip them one by one and see how fast you can count ¥100 million.

The rules are simple: tap the bill to flip it. That's it.
The timer starts the moment you flip the first bill, and you finish when all 10,000 are counted.

■ THE THRILL OF COUNTING ¥100 MILLION
• Bills fly off the stack with every tap
• Every 100 bills (¥1 million) fills a bundle — with the clunk of a cash-counting machine
• A celebration every ¥10 million
• Live amount, bill count, time and speed (bills per second)

■ CHASE YOUR RECORD
• Your personal best is saved automatically
• Earn a title based on your time — from Steady Bookkeeper to Legendary Cash Counter
• Share your result with friends

■ GOLD BILLS
• Watch an ad to play your next round with shining gold bills (cosmetic only — it does not affect your time)

■ FEATURES
• Auto-pauses when you switch apps (the timer stops too)
• Plays offline (ads and sharing need a connection)
• English and Japanese
